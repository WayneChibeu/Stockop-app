Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create a StockOp account, we collect your email address and profile name to authenticate you and secure your store data.

Store Data: We store your inventory, sales, suppliers, and shift reports in our secure cloud database (Firebase) to ensure your data syncs across devices.

2. Camera Access (Barcode Scanning)

StockOp requires access to your device's camera strictly for scanning product barcodes.

  • The camera feed is processed entirely locally on your device.
  • No images, photos, or video recordings are ever captured, saved, or transmitted to our servers.
  • The camera turns off automatically as soon as the barcode is scanned or the scanner modal is closed.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to cache your inventory and allow the app to work flawlessly offline. This data is kept strictly on your device until a connection is restored.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the StockOp service to you (inventory management, shift reporting, and checkout features). We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Push Notifications

If you opt-in to low stock alerts, your device token is used purely to deliver those native alerts. You can revoke this permission at any time in your browser or device settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Store Administrator or email us at support@stockop.app.
